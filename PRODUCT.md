## Stack

Static site in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript (no framework, no build step), responsive. Confirmed by the user. Domain not yet purchased: `https://servimangueras.com` is a placeholder used for canonical URL, sitemap and Open Graph, kept in one place so it can be swapped.

## Users

- Maintenance chiefs, mechanics and operators of yellow machinery (excavators, drilling rigs, agricultural and earth-moving equipment) in Barrancabermeja and the Magdalena Medio oil/industrial region, who need a hose assembled, a hydraulic component repaired or a part found, often urgently with a machine stopped.
- Purchasing staff at contractor and oil-sector companies comparing local suppliers and checking capabilities.

## Product Purpose

Public website for SERVIMANGUERAS — Repuestos y Suministros S.A.S. It replaces handing out the PDF brochure: it states who they are, what they repair and supply, gives technical hose information, and gets the visitor to call / WhatsApp or drive to the shop. Success = calls, WhatsApp messages and visits to the workshop.

## Positioning

Local hydraulic workshop since 2019 that combines hose assembly with repair of the components on the machine (pumps, motors, cylinders, Epiroc drilling and hammer equipment), machining, hydrostatic testing and 24-hour service.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services (brochure, verbatim scope): hydraulic review and maintenance of yellow machinery; repair of hydraulic components; assembly of hydraulic and industrial hoses; hydrostatic testing; machining (lathe and milling); maintenance of Epiroc DTH/TH drilling equipment models T35, T40, D60, D65; design and fabrication of metal structures; repair of Epiroc hydraulic hammers SB, MB, HB; repair/maintenance of hydraulic power-transmission components (pumps, motors, controls, cylinders, orbitrol) from Sauer Danfoss, Vickers, Parker, Rexroth and others, for agricultural, earth-moving and oil machinery; 3D modelling and ABS 3D printing; 24-hour service.

Products (brochure): ball/shut-off valves, fittings (racores), metric and inch O-rings, silicone hoses, quick couplings for air / prestolock, industrial belts.

Commercial support brands (brochure): Parker, Best Flex, Eaton, Alfagomma, Jason Industrial, Rexon.
